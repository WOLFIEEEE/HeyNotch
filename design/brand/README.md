## Original generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand.
Create a polished, original logo for "HeyNotch", an independent macOS app that makes the MacBook notch a useful little workspace for tasks, music and meeting notes. The brand is calm, clever, friendly and precise. This is the final flat logo artwork, not a mockup or a sheet of concepts.

Design direction: one distinctive compact blue emblem on the left, with the exact wordmark "HeyNotch" on the right. The emblem is an elegant notch silhouette: a straight horizontal top with subtle outward shoulders, softly rounded lower corners, and a broad sculpted lower edge. Work a bold, immediately readable capital H into its negative space. Make the H's vertical cuts broad enough to survive at a tiny app-icon size. Give the upper-right contour one small, purposeful stepped lift so the silhouette feels like a greeting and is recognizable even without the lettering. Resolve these ideas into ONE confident simple shape, with balanced mass and clean negative space, not a collection of objects. No detached decorative dots.

Wordmark: spell exactly "HeyNotch", with capital H and N. Beautiful carefully spaced modern humanist geometric sans lettering, medium/semibold weight, soft but precise terminals, calm optical kerning. It should feel at home on macOS without copying Apple's logo or using any Apple symbol. Dark ink #172333 wordmark. Emblem solid cobalt blue #3975E7. Flat 2-color artwork, no gradients, no material effects or shadows.

Composition: one horizontal icon-plus-wordmark lockup, centered on a wide canvas, generous transparent margins, emblem optically balanced with the wordmark, mark roughly 1.5 times cap height. Transparent background; all negative-space cutouts genuinely transparent. Crisp clean vector-like edges, no texture. No tagline, no explanations, no specimens, no swatches, no border, no 3D, no perspective, no sparkles, no microphone/headphone/checkmark/robot face, no watermark. Make it refined and distinctive rather than a generic rounded-square technology logo.

## Standalone symbol edit prompt

Use case: precise-object-edit / logo-brand.
Input image is the newly designed HeyNotch logo. Create its standalone emblem asset.
Keep the exact silhouette of the cobalt blue notch-shaped H emblem from the LEFT of this image: wide top ledge, small raised top-right shoulder, flared sides that curve into the generous rounded lower edge, and the bold H-shaped transparent cutout. Preserve its distinctive proportions and all intentional contours. Remove the entire HeyNotch wordmark. Center only the emblem on a square transparent canvas, with the emblem occupying about 75% of the canvas width. Keep its original approximately 1.6:1 width-to-height proportion; do not stretch it to a square.
Refine the emblem to a clean uniform solid cobalt #3975E7 fill, with smooth precisely antialiased edges. No gradient, no shading, no glow, no tiny stray marks. The background and the internal H cutout must have actual alpha transparency. No square app-icon tile behind it, no text, no caption, no swatches, no mockup. This is a clean reusable logo symbol, vector-like flat geometry rendered as high-resolution PNG.
