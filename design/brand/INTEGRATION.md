A softly rounded square off-white porcelain tile with restrained dimensionality, very subtle edge shading, no strong cast shadow; the tile occupies about 88% of the canvas with consistent transparent outer margins. Place the approved blue emblem centered on the tile, occupying about 66% of the full canvas width, naturally wide and short in its original proportions. The H cutout should show the same off-white tile underneath. Crisp edges and excellent contrast. Quiet, clean, modern macOS utility icon.
No text, no letters other than the existing negative-space H, no extra dots, no decorative sparkle, no new badges, no mockup scene. Actual transparency around the rounded-square tile. High resolution square app icon master.
